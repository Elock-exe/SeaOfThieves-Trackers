Voyages & Trading Companies

Quick reference for voyage types and the six companies tracked in every pirate's history.

Voyage Types

Gold Hoarders Voyage

Riddle or X-marks maps leading to buried chests. Best run solo or duo on a sloop.

Order of Souls Bounty

Skeleton captain hunts across islands and skeleton ships. Bring blunderbombs for crowd control.

Merchant Alliance Delivery

Fragile cargo runs — livestock, crates or cannons. Watch your ship's tilt.

Athena's Fortune Voyage

Combined multi-company voyages with the highest emissary value.

Reaper's Chest Hunt

No map — track down a randomly placed chest and turn it in at a Reaper's hideout.

Fort of the Damned

Server-wide PvPvE event. High risk, high reward loot from the skeleton fleet.

Trading Companies

Gold Hoarders

Treasure maps and buried chests. The classic entry point for new crews.

Order of Souls

Bounty hunting against the undead — skeleton captains, forts and fleets.

Merchant Alliance

Cargo delivery and animal transport contracts across the map.

Athena's Fortune

Unified high-stakes voyages requiring standing with the other companies.

Reaper's Bones

PvP-focused faction rewarding stolen loot and rival ship kills.

Hunter's Call

Fishing and cooking contracts, plus megalodon and kraken trophies.
