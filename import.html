Import your account

One click, nothing to install. Publishes your gold, Hourglass rank, reputation and commendations.

  1. 1

    Drag this to your bookmarks bar

    It is a bookmark that runs a script. Not a download, not an extension.

    ⚓ Import SoT Profile

    No bookmarks bar? Press Ctrl+Shift+B (Cmd+Shift+B on Mac).

  2. 2

    Open your Sea of Thieves account

    Sign in with your Xbox account and go to your profile overview.

    seaofthieves.com/profile/overview

  3. 3

    Click the bookmark

    An overlay shows the progress, and gives you a link to your profile when it is done.

On a phone, or no drag and drop
  1. Bookmark any page, for example google.com.
  2. Edit that bookmark.
  3. Replace its address with the code below.
  4. Open your Sea of Thieves account, then open that bookmark.

Phone browsers handle bookmarklets badly. If it opens a new tab instead of running, this route will not work there and the extension is the better option.

What this does, and what it cannot do
  • Unofficial. Not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Rare or Microsoft.
  • It reads, nothing else. The script asks seaofthieves.com for the same profile data the page is already showing you, and sends only those game stats to the tracker.
  • It never sees your password. It cannot: it runs after you are already signed in, and it never reads or copies your session cookie. The browser attaches that, we never see its value.
  • It only runs when you click it. Nothing stays behind, nothing runs in the background, nothing watches your other tabs.
  • It can only see your own profile. There is no way for it to read anybody else’s.
  • Published stats are public. Anyone who knows your pirate name can look them up.

Read the exact code that runs. It is not minified and nothing is hidden: importer.js

Want it to happen on its own?

This imports when you click it, and not otherwise. The browser extension does the same thing by itself, roughly once an hour, so your profile stays current without you thinking about it.
